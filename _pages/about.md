I am a Ph.D. student in [Electrical Engineering at POSTECH](https://eee.postech.ac.kr/) (since February 2026), advised by [Prof. Hyemin Ahn](https://sites.google.com/view/hyemin-ahn) in the Robot Learning Lab (RLLab). My research focuses on **robotic manipulation** and **world models** for intelligent robot control. I am also interested in robotic foundation models.

I earned my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at [UNIST](https://www.unist.ac.kr/) (March 2024–February 2026), where I worked in the AI-HRI Lab with Prof. Ahn. Before that, I earned my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from [Myongji University](https://www.mju.ac.kr/) (March 2018–February 2024) and worked in the Dynamic Robot Control Lab (DRCL) under [Prof. Dong-il Choi](https://www.mju.ac.kr/mjukr/253/subview.do?enc=Zm5jdDF8QEB8JTJGcHJvZiUyRm1qdWtyJTJGMTQ0JTJGNjA0NDIlMkZ2aWV3LmRvJTNG).

## Technical report

**[Demonstrating a Vision-Based AI Robot for Strategic Board Games](https://sites.google.com/view/gomoku-robot/)** — Taehwan Kim<sup>&#42;</sup>, Dokeun Lee<sup>&#42;</sup>, <strong>Seonghyeon Kim</strong><sup>&#42;</sup>, Yongjun Choi<sup>&#42;</sup>, Sungjun Heo, Thi Thuy Ngan Duong, Kyungdon Joo, Namhun Kim, Jeong hwan Jeon, and Hyemin Ahn. *The first four authors contributed equally.* This technical report describes a low-cost Gomoku robot integrating real-time vision, reinforcement learning decisions, and robotic arm control.
